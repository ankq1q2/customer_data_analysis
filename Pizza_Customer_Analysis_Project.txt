
EASTERN COAST PIZZA CUSTOMER ANALYSIS - PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

PROBLEM STATEMENT

A pizza restaurant chain along the eastern coast has collected
customer data (region, pizza type, visit frequency, average
spend, visit time, review ratings) but lacks a systematic way to
interpret it for decision-making. Without visibility into which
pizza types drive revenue, which regions bring the most
customers, when peak sales occur, and how customers rate
different products, the business cannot make informed decisions
on menu optimization, staffing, marketing, or quality
improvement.

OBJECTIVES

1. Analyze customer distribution across regions.
2. Study distribution of spending and review ratings.
3. Explore visit frequency vs. average spend by pizza type.
4. Identify revenue patterns across pizza type and visit time.
5. Rank pizza types by total revenue.
6. Evaluate satisfaction (mean/median/mode ratings) per pizza type.
7. Derive actionable business insights.

WORKFLOW

1. Setup: Import numpy, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn; set theme.
2. Data Cleaning: Load CSV, strip column whitespace, standardize
   Region text, create TotalRevenue = AvgSpend x VisitFrequency.
3. Univariate EDA: Pie chart of region share; histograms (KDE) of
   AvgSpend and Review.
4. Bivariate EDA: Scatter plot of Visit Frequency vs. AvgSpend,
   colored by Pizza Type.
5. Revenue Analysis: Heatmap of revenue by Pizza Type x Visit
   Time; bar chart ranking revenue by Pizza Type.
6. Satisfaction Analysis: Grouped bar chart of mean/median/mode
   ratings per Pizza Type.
7. Reporting: Print top/bottom pizza, peak time slot, best
   pizza-time combo, and full ratings table.
8. Conclusion: Summarize findings into business recommendations.

CONCLUSION: CUSTOMER BEHAVIOUR INSIGHTS

1. Satisfaction Drives Revenue: Meat Lovers is the top revenue
   pizza (Rs. 75,602), while White Pizza is both the lowest
   revenue (Rs. 26,198) and lowest rated (mean 1.46, median &
   mode 1.0) - low satisfaction is directly suppressing repeat
   purchases and spend.

2. Dinner Is the Dominant Occasion: Dinner drives the highest
   revenue overall (Rs. 186,002), with Meat Lovers at Dinner alone
   contributing Rs. 41,831 - pizza is primarily an evening meal
   choice, guiding staffing and promotion timing.

3. Ratings Are Stable Except One Outlier: Most pizza types
   (BBQ Chicken, Buffalo Chicken, Hawaiian, Margherita, Meat
   Lovers, Pepperoni, Veggie Supreme) cluster around 3.5-4.0 mean
   rating, showing a consistently satisfied customer base. White
   Pizza is a clear outlier needing recipe/pricing review.

4. Spend and Loyalty Vary by Product: The frequency-vs-spend
   scatter shows some pizza types attract frequent low-spend
   customers while others attract infrequent high-spend
   customers - useful for designing loyalty programs vs. premium
   upsells.

5. Regional Concentration: Customer share is not evenly spread
   across regions, suggesting marketing/expansion should focus on
   strong regions while underperforming ones may need pricing or
   competition review.

Overall, customer behaviour tracks closely with satisfaction:
high ratings sustain revenue (Meat Lovers), poor ratings erode it
(White Pizza), and Dinner is the strongest, most consistent
behavioural pattern anchoring the customer base.
